Optional Runner Extras

  • Lateral band walks: 1–2 sets
  • Step-downs or step-ups: 1–2 x 6–8/side
  • Tib raises: 1–2 x 12–20
  • Dead bug or side plank: 1–2 sets
  • Light pogo hops: 1–2 x 15–20

Use these as optional add-ons after strength or before runs if you want extra runner-specific support.

Strength During the 5K Block

  • Keep 2 sessions per week: enough to maintain strength, not enough to sabotage quality running.
  • Cut volume, not all intensity: 2–3 work sets on the big lifts is usually enough.
  • Main idea: one squat-based day and one hinge-based day, both with a press and a pull.
  • If running quality drops: reduce gym volume before you panic about your fitness.

Plyometric Warm-Up Ideas

  • Before quality run days: after the easy jog and drills, add 2–3 of the following in small doses.
  • Pogo hops: 2 x 15–20 contacts
  • A-skips: 2 x 20m
  • Skipping for height or distance: 2 x 20m
  • Bounds or power skips: 2 x 20m if you feel coordinated and fresh
  • Rule: these should make you feel springy, not tired.

Session Philosophy

  • Threshold day: controlled and repeatable, not dramatic.
  • 5K day: more specific and sharper, but still not a string of mini-races.
  • Long run: long enough to support the 5K, not so long it kills your legs.
  • Progression logic: build volume first, then gently extend rep length.
