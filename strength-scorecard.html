Strength Scorecard

A simple way to judge whether strength is improving, holding, or needs another build phase

Use this every 8–12 weeks: look at trends, not perfection.

How to use it

  • Review every block: strength block, 5K block, 10K block, etc.
  • Look for direction, not drama: improving, holding steady, or slipping badly.
  • Use both numbers and feel: the barbell matters, but so do niggles, stability, and confidence.
  • Main question: can you maintain a decent strength base while running well, or do you need another build phase?

Scorecard

Area What to track Good signs Warning signs
Squat Working weight, form, confidence, consistency Weights trend upward or hold steady; movement feels solid Repeated stalls, confidence dropping, knee irritation rising

Bench / OHP Working weight, bar speed, tolerance of pressing volume Slow steady progress or easy maintenance during run blocks Pressing collapses quickly once running rises
Row / Pull Row quality, pulldown weight, pull-up assistance level More control, less assistance needed, better upper-back strength No progress at all, poor control, shoulders feel cranky
Pull-up goal Dead hangs, eccentrics, assisted reps, first unassisted rep Assistance decreases over time; first rep gets closer Stuck for ages despite consistent work
Single-leg stability Split squats, step-downs, step-ups, wobbliness More stable, less knee wobble, stronger control each side Persistent instability, big side-to-side difference, knee cave issues
Calves / lower leg Calf raise consistency, soleus strength, tolerance of loading Can handle regular calf work and running without flare-ups Recurring lower-leg soreness, poor tolerance, skipped work
Knee robustness Front-of-knee niggles, tolerance of hills, runs, and lifting Less awareness, fewer flare-ups, better control when tired Niggles recur often or show up earlier as load rises

Simple decision rule

Maintenance is probably enough if most of these are true:

  • Your main lifts are holding steady or slowly improving
  • Your knee is mostly calm and running feels stable
  • You feel more robust, not more fragile
  • Pull-up progress is still moving, even slowly
  • Two strength sessions per week keep most of the gains

You probably need another strength-building phase if several of these are true:

  • Your lifts are clearly sliding
  • You feel wobbly or underpowered
  • Knee niggles keep returning
  • Strength drops fast whenever running increases
  • You don’t feel like you’ve actually built enough yet

Most important mindset

You are not trying to become “strong enough forever” in one go. You’re trying to build a base that supports your running, health, confidence, and future self. The scorecard is there to help you notice whether you’re trending upward, holding well, or due for another build.
