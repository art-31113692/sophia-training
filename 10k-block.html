Strength During the 10K Block

  • Keep 2 sessions per week: enough to maintain strength, not enough to sabotage the running.
  • Lower the cost when needed: the closer you get to peak running weeks, the more ruthless you should be about accessory fluff.
  • Main idea: one squat-based day and one hinge-based day, both with a press and a pull.
  • This is maintenance: strength should support the race build, not compete with it.
  • If running quality drops: cut gym volume first.

Session Philosophy

  • Threshold day: the anchor of the week. Calm, repeatable, and durable.
  • 10K day: specific enough to build race rhythm, but never a weekly time trial.
  • Long run: more important here than in the 5K block, but still not a secret third workout.
  • Optional quality in the long run: just a small steady finish in some weeks, not threshold buried inside the long run.
  • Big-picture logic: this block is about extending fitness, not proving toughness.
  • Pacing rule: if you start dreading the sessions or need hero mode to complete them, back off a notch.
